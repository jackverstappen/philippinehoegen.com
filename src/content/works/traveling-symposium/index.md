#### **Gathering amidst the ruins: on the potential of assembly within the context of art institutions**

#### Symposium on tour 2021/2022

#### On new ways of working together in the art world

27 october 2021

[<u>https://clubsolo.nl/agenda/casco-art-institutegathering-amidst-the-ruins-over-de-potentie-van-samenkomen-binnen-kunstinstellingen/?archive=1</u>](https://clubsolo.nl/agenda/casco-art-institutegathering-amidst-the-ruins-over-de-potentie-van-samenkomen-binnen-kunstinstellingen/?archive=1)

In 2020, Club Solo published the book [on the future and the artist-run spaces](https://clubsolo.us9.list-manage.com/track/click?u=50d48ca1bc0ea95d0acba4cd3&id=b69802a446&e=2e1d8c60b5). This was followed by the [podcast series](https://clubsolo.us9.list-manage.com/track/click?u=50d48ca1bc0ea95d0acba4cd3&id=c9054d8262&e=2e1d8c60b5) with the same title and the online exchanges with the Master Institute of Visual Cultures in Breda. With the symposium on tour, we continue the cooperation with writers and speakers ‘physically’ on location.

The first evening is compiled and organised by **Casco Art Institute**: Working for the Commons.

Casco Art Institute is an experimental platform where art invites a new vision of society. Art and commons are Casco’s two most important pillars. They are means for a better way of living together. The commons can be described as the natural and cultural resources held in common by a community, for example, community gardens, mutual aid networks, and open source software. Art is in fact inherent to the commons, as they are shared resources to keep the culture of community alive. In turn, the commons may well sustain art.

For Casco, ‘coming together’; is an important instrument to determine in which direction the institution should go. But which forms of coming together lead to an open and layered decision-making process? What happens if you choose other shapes? How relevant is coming together for the art practice?

In order not to discuss the subject in a judgmental way, the evening takes the form of a ‘lexicon’ composed of words that can make a gathering meaningful and artistically inspiring. Participants in the conversation: Jeanine van Berkel, Hodan Warsame and Tomi Hilsee. Introduction by Ying and Annette Kraus. Philippine Hoegen is the moderator of the evening.

English spoken

location: In De Ruimte (IDR)\
Oudegracht 230, Utrecht (at the wharf)

# **Symposium on Tour: How Do We Proceed from Here?**

#### 14 May 2022 

#### Image: courtesy of Falke Pisano as part of the project ‘Where Shall We Begin?’, currently on view at M HKA, Antwerp

#### On Saturday May 14 2022, Club Solo’s Symposium on Tour will be **hosted by TENT** .

[<u>https://clubsolo.nl/agenda/learning-to-share-een-werksessie-over-andere-manieren-van-samenwerken/?archive=1</u>](https://clubsolo.nl/agenda/learning-to-share-een-werksessie-over-andere-manieren-van-samenwerken/?archive=1)

#### This gathering departs from emerging theoretical and experiential knowledges with regard to labour and value producing mechanisms in the field of art. During **How Do We Proceed From Here?**, speakers, respondents, and audiences are invited to navigate some of the paradoxes in the politics of labour and value producing mechanisms in the arts that are perceived, for example, in the nexus between (in)visibility, speculation, and narrative.

#### With a growing, collective desire for sustainability, justice, and solidarity in the arts – especially in the present moment and increasingly anchored in policies – this exchange aims to nurture and add to these critical issues and queries in their emancipatory potential. Concretely, it will do so by foregrounding the ongoing precarity as experienced by divergent workers, some of the intricacies surrounding creating socially safe working environments, and finally, dealing with tensions and discomfort. Throughout, the aim is to arrive at various possible answers to the following questions: What preconditions are required when working collectively and creatively in, through, and with institutions\* in different capacities? Which alliances are available and practiced, and which are potentially foreclosed? And lastly ‘What happens when labour becomes not just a thematic or an image for artistic production, but when artistic production is re-imagined as itself a form of labour? What kind of politics might this lead to?’\*\*

#### Invited speakers are artists and educators Sami Hammana and Falke Pisano. Respondents include activist and academic Zouhair Hammana and researcher Yue Mao. Curator of the event Katayoun Arian will join the conversation. Moderation by Philippine Hoegen.

#### **How Do We Proceed From Here?** is part of Symposium on Tour, a series of events initiated by Club Solo around the subject of how we work together in the arts. Earlier iterations took place at Casco Art institute, Utrecht and Kunsthal Gent.

#### \*Here, the term ‘institutions’ \[of art\] is understood both as foundations, that is, organised entities with boards, eligible to receiving municipal and national funds for the purpose of making artistic programmes, and as formally acknowledged graduate and post-graduate educational organisations.\
\*Vishmidt, Marina, Speculation as a Mode of Production: Forms of Value Subjectivity in Art and Capital, 2018, p. 135.

# **Reizend Symposium over nieuwe vormen van samenwerken in de kunstwereld**

#### 4 June 2022 

#### 

#### Symposium on Tour ‘on new forms of collaboration in the art world’ is initiated by Club Solo. Previous editions took place in Casco Art Institute (Utrecht), Kunsthal Gent and TENT Welcomes (curator Katayoun Arian, Rotterdam). On Sunday June 19, the final episode takes place **in Club Solo, Breda.**

#### [<u>https://clubsolo.nl/agenda/reizend-symposium-over-nieuwe-vormen-van-samenwerken-in-de-kunstwereld/?archive=1</u>](https://clubsolo.nl/agenda/reizend-symposium-over-nieuwe-vormen-van-samenwerken-in-de-kunstwereld/?archive=1)

#### During this afternoon, subjects that were leading in the previous episodes, will be presented to representatives of artists’ initiatives and other art institutions, who will draw from their own institutions, who will enter into discussion on the basis of their own experiences and insights. The following questions are central: How do we talk to each other? How do we work together? Where do we want to go and how do we do that?

#### The afternoon will consist of three consecutive parts, in which we will take the forms of the previous episodes as a starting point:\
1) assembly – the form of Casco Art Institute\
2) working tables – the form of Kunsthal Gent\
3) panel debate – the form of Katayoun Arian in TENT Welcomes

#### For this programme, the Club Solo building will be furnished by LU’UM, an open collective consisting of architects, urban planners, designers and artists. Moderator is Philippine Hoegen.

#### The symposium comes out of a series of programs on the future of artists’ initiatives that Club Solo previously organized. We published the book about the future and artist initiatives, which was followed by a series of podcasts and master classes at the Institute for Visual Cultures Breda.

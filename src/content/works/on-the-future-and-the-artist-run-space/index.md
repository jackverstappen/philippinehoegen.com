# **Dreams as a guideline** 67 MIN, Dutch spoken

### EPISODE 1

Club Solo presents a podcast symposium in three episodes, as a follow-up to the recently launched book [on the future and the artist-run spaces](https://clubsoloshop.myonline.store/a-60255386/boeken/on-the-future-and-the-artist-run-spaces-english-version/#description). The language of the first two episodes is Dutch. The [third one](https://soundcloud.com/club-solo/over-de-toekomst-en-de-kunstenaarsinitiatieven-3-samenwerken-als-drijfveer) is in English.

In the first episode, ‘dreams as a guideline’, Maria Barnas, reinaart vanhoe and Lotte van Geijn are invited. They question whether there can exist a culture in which we all feel at home and can meet each other. Is language that common space? Can the space of language merge with the physical space of an artists’ initiative?

Moderator of the conversation is Philippine Hoegen, researcher and artist. The episode concludes with the sound work *Soundshine File \#990012* by Maria Barnas and Nathalie Bruys.

**Viable theories** 52 MIN, Dutch spoken


### EPISODE 2

Club Solo presents a podcast symposium in three episodes, as a follow-up to the recently launched book [on the future and the artist-run spaces](https://clubsoloshop.myonline.store/a-60255386/boeken/on-the-future-and-the-artist-run-spaces-english-version/#description). The language of the first two episodes is Dutch. The [third one](https://soundcloud.com/club-solo/over-de-toekomst-en-de-kunstenaarsinitiatieven-3-samenwerken-als-drijfveer) is in English.

In the second episode, ‘viable theories’, Jack Segbars, Rosa Paardenkooper, and Katayoun Arian are invited. They discuss, among other things, the influence of the pandemic on the art world. Are we looking for more solidarity? Will there be room for new ways of working? Or will we just pick up where we left off, within the same system, in the same roles and with the same production goals?

# **Working together as a driving force** 43 MIN, english spoken

### EPISODE 3

Club Solo presents a podcast symposium in three episodes, as a follow-up to the recently launched book [on the future and the artist-run spaces](https://clubsoloshop.myonline.store/a-60255386/boeken/on-the-future-and-the-artist-run-spaces-english-version/#description). The language of this third episode is English.

In the third episode, ‘working together as a driving force’, Annie Jael Kwan, Binna Choi and Christopher Kennedy are invited. From Singapore, Utrecht and New York, they discuss the changes their work and lives are undergoing as a result of the pandemic. Are their pre-March 2020 ideals still the guiding principle, or are they opting for a different way of working, with a view to other goals? Is cooperation on a global scale still possible now that we almost only see each other on screen?

Philippine Hoegen, researcher and artist, will moderate the discussion. The episode concludes with the poem *Loose* by Ester Naomi Perquin.

***Callisthenics, Stories of Support, Aversion and Love*** is a performance by Paloma Bouhanna, Henny Dörr, and Philippine Hoegen, made in 2025. It is both non-verbal and textual: the score is based on a series of letters the three performers wrote to each other, and the movement vocabulary explores physically what is being addressed in those letters — to feel and enact something bodily, and to make that doing and feeling visible. The score is handed out to the public after each performance, it includes all the letters, and the movement vocabulary in the form of drawings.
